# FICIAI 2026: Executive Overview & Strategic Synthesis

## 1. What is FICIAI?

**FICIAI** stands for the **Forum International du Cinéma, de l'Intelligence Artificielle et de l'Investissement** (*International Forum on Cinema, AI and Investment*).

It is an international forum scheduled to take place from **November 6 to November 8, 2026**, in **Ouarzazate, Morocco**. FICIAI serves as the annual flagship event of the broader territorial transformation program: **"Ouarzazate Smart City of Cinema 2030" (OSC 2030)**.

---

## 2. Core Vision & Purpose

The initiative is designed to transition Ouarzazate from being solely an iconic international filming location into an integrated, forward-looking creative, technological, and investment hub.

* **Human-Centric AI Integration:** Artificial intelligence is positioned not to replace artists, but as a catalyst for human creativity, local talent emancipation, technical upskilling, and virtual production competitiveness.
* **Smart & Sustainable Infrastructure:** Leveraging Ouarzazate's natural film heritage, geographic position, and clean energy assets (such as the Noor Solar Complex) to establish Africa's first cinematic smart city.
* **Economic & Diplomatic Bridge:** Connecting international film studios, AI research institutions, venture capital, and local youth/craftsmen across Africa, Europe, Asia, and the Americas.

## 4. Strategic Axes & Pillars

1. **Cinematic Heritage Preservation:** Safeguarding traditional filmmaking memory and artisanal craftsmanship.
2. **AI & Virtual Production Innovation:** Deploying cutting-edge tools (motion capture, digital twins, generative AI workflows) in service of storytelling.
3. **Training & Youth Employability:** Practical skill transfer and capacity building for Moroccan and African youth.
4. **Creative Investment & Entrepreneurship:** Structuring financing pipelines with international funds and private equity.
5. **Cultural Diplomacy & South-South Cooperation:** Strengthening transnational artistic and institutional alliances.

---

## 5. Expected Outcomes & Deliverables

* **Déclaration de Ouarzazate 2026:** Adoption of the foundational charter for OSC 2030.
* **Operational Roadmap 2026–2030:** Execution framework across 22 structural projects (urban scenography, smart city technology, talent academies, industrial production hubs, lifestyle appeal).
* **Livre Blanc Ouarzazate 2030:** A post-forum strategic white paper for stakeholders and policymakers.
* **Cinema Legacy Awards & Recognition:** Official honors dedicated to behind-the-scenes film workers (*"Héros de l’Ombre"*).
* **Capital Mobilization:** Active investment facilitation with global film funds (e.g., CineNow $150M fund) and private offices.
